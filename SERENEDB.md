# SereneDB fork

Based on FreeType 2.14.1 (`526ec5c47b9ebccc4754c85ac0c0cdf7c85a5e9b`).

SereneDB requires identical recursive and nonrecursive dependency checkouts.
The optional nested `dlg` submodule has been removed; `FT_DEBUG_LOGGING` is
unsupported in this fork. The FreeType license and copyright notices are
unchanged.
